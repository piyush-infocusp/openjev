# Gemma 4 E2B/E4B multimodal facts (https://gemma.ai/models/gemma-4/model-card/, fetched 2026-11-19; ai.google.dev model_card_4 redirects there)
- E2B: 2.3B effective, 5.1B with embeddings, 35 layers. E4B: 4.5B effective, 8B with embeddings, 42 layers. Vision encoder ~150M, audio encoder ~300M. 512-token sliding window interleaved with global layers, last layer global; Per-Layer Embeddings (lookup tables). 128K context. Text, image, audio. Audio max 30 s; video max 60 s at 1 fps. Apache 2.0. The page had no memory table.
- Memory (from search snippet quoting Google's table, approximate, unverified on the page): E2B 11.4 GB BF16, 5.7 GB 8-bit, 2.9 GB 4-bit; E4B 17.9 / 8.9 / 4.5 GB. Another third party: E2B 9.6 GB BF16. LiteRT-LM loads vision/audio components only when needed.
- T4 (16 GB, no native bf16): towers alone ~0.45B params = ~0.9 GB fp16. Training memory is dominated by activation length of image/audio tokens, not tower weights (own estimate, not measured).
- Related: T5Gemma 2 keeps the Gemma 3 vision encoder frozen (snippet); BidirLM-Omni-2.5B (text, vision, audio, arXiv 2604.02045, Gemma3 / Qwen3 based, CC BY 4.0 paper; model licence unverified). LLM-as-Jev reports frozen LLMs handle multimodal decisions (MMBench Apache-2.0, MMStar licence not stated).
- Datasets: MINDS-14 (PolyAI/minds14, CC-BY-4.0, 14 intents, ~600 clips x 14 languages); SLURP (60 intents, 18 domains, 16,521 utterances per MASSIVE card; licence unverified); Mind2Web (715k episodes / 30k instructions per CogAgent paper, 6.4k Multimodal-Mind2Web samples per ScreenSuite table), AndroidControl (15,000 tasks, 833 apps; licence unverified), ScreenSpot (1,272 items; licence unverified).
